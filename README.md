## Overview
For my final project, I created a simple food catching video game. I decided to use a pixelated style to match the simplistic mechanics of the game, which I designed and created on [Piskel](piskelapp.com). 

To play, move across the screen using the arrow keys, catch fresh food, and serve it for points. The player has to catch all the food, and is given three chances if any of the fresh food is not caught. Rotten fish also fall down on occasion, but be careful not to catch the fish! If any fish are caught, the game is over. 

There is a home screen with a play and quit button, as well as instructions for how to play. When the player loses, a game over screen is shown, with options to quit and retry. 

Overall, I believe that my final project was a success. There were a few things (such as the customers) that I did not have enough time to implement, but the gameplay still works without them there. I also wish I had more time to work on the artwork in the game, so in the future I might go back and edit the sprites and backgrounds.
