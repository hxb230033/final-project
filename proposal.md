# final project: Bakery food stacking game
## link to GitHub repository: https://github.com/hxb230033/final-project.git
## I will create a simple food stacking game. The player will attempt to catch falling food to serve customers without letting any of the food fall.

### Features of the game:
 - a main character that moves left and right
 - falling food that can be caught be the player: uncaught food is penalized
 - serving station to give customers food: earns the player points
### Challenges in making the game:
 - I will need to learn how to make the character move left to right, collect falling items, and put them down.
 - I will also have to research how to make more complex visual elements for the character, food, and background.
### Ideal outcome:
 - The ideal outcome will be a  minigame with falling food that can be caught and given away to customers for points.
 - The game will give the player three chances if any food is not caught. 
 - The gameplay will gradually get harder as the time goes on(more food will fall, and it will be faster).
 - There will be music and sound effects.
### Minimal viable outcome:
- The minimal outcome will be a minigame with falling, catchable food.
- The game will award points for each individual food item caught if the customer point system cannot be implemented.
- The player will get three chances if food is not caught: from there, the game will end.
